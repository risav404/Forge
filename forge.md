##A platform that accepts jobs, queues them, executes them through workers, and reports their results.

Core engineering challenges:
- Job queues and worker architecture
- Scheduling and concurrency
- Retries and failure recovery
- Process management
- PostgreSQL and APIs
- Logs and observability
